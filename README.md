> *"Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication — build tools that work quietly and well."*

I'm a **Cybersecurity student** and **Linux systems enthusiast** from Ethiopia, obsessed with understanding how systems work at a deep level — and then breaking them apart to build something better.

My work sits at the intersection of **security**, **automation**, and **open-source tooling**. I write Bash and Python scripts that eliminate repetitive work, build platforms that help others learn security, and spend an unhealthy amount of time perfecting my Linux environment.

When I'm not hacking on something, I'm refining configs, studying low-level networking, or figuring out why my Hyprland setup still has one pixel out of place.

**What drives me:**
- Designing tools that are reliable, minimal, and actually useful
- Making cybersecurity knowledge more accessible — especially in Ethiopia
- Understanding systems deeply enough to know exactly where they break
